# Closed Test Kit: Pitty Pat

Personal Play Console accounts need **12+ testers opted in for 14 consecutive days** before Production unlocks. Testers who opt out and back in restart their 14 days. See `play-console-checklist.md` for sources.

## Plan

- **Recruit 15-18 people**, not 12. A few will drop out, and one drop-out near day 13 can cost you days.
- **Testers need a Google account and an Android phone.** iPhone users can't join an Android closed test.
- **Ship at least one update during the 14 days** (even a small one). The production application asks what you changed based on feedback.
- **Ask for real play**, not just installs: the application asks how testers engaged.

## Timeline

| Day | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0 | First signed build uploaded to the **Closed testing** track; testers get the opt-in link |
| 1-3 | Everyone installs; you check that 12+ show as opted in |
| 3-7 | Testers play, try the daily challenge, buy something in the Locker; feedback form open |
| ~7 | Ship update 1.0.0+2 with fixes from feedback |
| 14+ | Apply for production (answers drafted from the feedback below) |

## Invite message (copy, then edit to taste)

> Hey! I built a mobile version of Pitty Pat and I need a few people to test it on Android before it goes live on Google Play.
>
> It takes about 2 minutes to join:
> 1. Open this link on your Android phone and tap "Become a tester": **[opt-in link from Play Console]**
> 2. Install Pitty Pat from the Play Store page it takes you to.
> 3. Please keep it installed and stay signed up for at least 2 weeks (Google requires this).
>
> Play a few matches, try the Daily Challenge, and tell me anything that feels off: **[feedback form link]**
>
> Thank you, this genuinely helps!

## Reminder (day 5 and day 10)

> Quick reminder: please keep Pitty Pat installed and stay signed up as a tester until **[date of day 14]**. If you have a minute, today's Daily Challenge is new, and I'd love your feedback here: **[feedback form link]**

## Feedback form (Google Forms)

1. Which phone do you have? (short answer)
2. How many matches have you played? (0 / 1-3 / 4-10 / 10+)
3. Did you try the Daily Challenge? (Yes / No)
4. Did you unlock anything in the Locker? (Yes / No / Didn't know it existed)
5. How fun is it, 1 to 5? (scale)
6. Which difficulty felt right? (Chill / Clever / Sharp / None of them)
7. Did anything break, freeze, look wrong or feel confusing? (paragraph)
8. Anything you'd add or change? (paragraph)
9. Would you keep playing after the test? (Yes / Maybe / No)

## Notes for the production application

Fill these in as the test runs; the application has three sections (closed test, the game, readiness).

- **Recruiting:** who you invited, how many joined, how many stayed 14 days.
- **Engagement:** matches played, daily challenge use, Locker use (from the form; Play Console also shows install counts).
- **Feedback themes:** top 3 issues and how each was handled.
- **Changes shipped:** list each update and what it fixed.
- **Readiness:** crash-free during the test (Play Console > Android vitals), all features exercised, feedback addressed.
